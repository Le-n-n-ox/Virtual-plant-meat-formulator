# FoodML texture-prediction data (2023)

This directory contains `foodml_texture_prediction_2023.csv`, the authors' 54-row sample-level data file used in Ata et al. (2023).

## Attribution and reuse basis

Kircali Ata, S.; Shi, J. K.; Yao, X.; Hua, X. Y.; Haldar, S.; Chiang, J. H.; Wu, M. (2023). “Predicting the Textural Properties of Plant-Based Meat Analogs with Machine Learning.” *Foods* 12(2), 344. <https://doi.org/10.3390/foods12020344>.

The article and supplement state a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license and that the experimental data are available in the article and supplement. This CSV is the authors' dataset associated with that work. The separate MIT license in the authors' repository applies to code; it is not treated as the data license.

The dataset includes source data from Chiang et al. (2019; DOI `10.1016/j.foostr.2018.11.002`), Chiang et al. (2021; DOI `10.1016/j.foostr.2021.100183`), and Ferawati et al. (2021; DOI `10.3390/foods10040843`). Preserve those attributions and the regime distinctions when reusing individual rows.

Original locations:

- Authors' repository: <https://github.com/sezinata/FoodML>
- Source CSV: <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sezinata/FoodML/main/data/additional_30_12_12.csv>

SHA-256: `03ad4b42a70c9ab375a602c2a786b4367e616c0fac271ebab5cd7a805148efa`.

The 54 rows comprise 42 high-moisture extrusion samples and 12 mechanical-elongation samples. The paper's published prediction errors cover this mixed dataset, not an HME-only subset or Kenyan formulations.
